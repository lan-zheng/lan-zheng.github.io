Help us understand the issue

If it helps, include:

  • Your device model and iOS version
  • Your WorldPin app version
  • What you expected and what happened
  • A screenshot with private details hidden, if useful
Please don’t email passwords, payment card details, or sensitive personal information. WorldPin support will never ask you to send your Apple password.

Restore a Passport purchase

Open the Passport screen in WorldPin and choose Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. If it still does not appear, email us with the purchase date and your app version. Do not send payment card information.

Manage your travel records

You can edit or delete a trip from its travel history. Removing a single visit does not remove another visit to that same place. Exported travel books and cards are files you control on your device.

恢复 Passport 购买

请在 WorldPin 的 Passport 页面点击“恢复购买”，并确认设备已登录购买时使用的 Apple 账户。如果仍未显示，请邮件告知购买日期和 App 版本。请勿发送银行卡信息。

管理旅行记录

你可以在旅行记录中编辑或删除单次旅程。删除某一次到访不会删除同一地点的其他到访记录。导出的旅行册和卡片是由你在设备上管理的文件。
