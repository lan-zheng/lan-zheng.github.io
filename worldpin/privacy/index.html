YOUR JOURNEY, YOUR DATA

Privacy
policy.

WorldPin is a travel journal and collection app. This policy describes how the current version handles information.

EFFECTIVE DATE · 19 OCTOBER 2026

In brief: Your travel records are stored in the app on your device. WorldPin does not operate an account service or a server that receives your travel journal. Apple provides map and payment services when you use those features.

Information you add

When you use WorldPin, you may enter countries and regions you have visited, trip locations, dates, notes, departure locations, and places on your wishlist. The app also saves settings such as your chosen character, language, and appearance. These records are stored locally in the app on your device.

Location and maps

WorldPin does not ask for access to your device's precise location in the current version. You enter destinations yourself. Map screens display geographic points selected by the app and use Apple MapKit to show map content. Apple may process technical information needed to provide that map service under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

One-time Passport purchases and restore requests are handled by Apple's App Store and StoreKit. WorldPin receives purchase entitlement status so it can unlock the purchased features. We do not receive your full payment card number. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing and exports

When you create a travel card, poster, GIF, or HTML travel book, the app generates the file on your device. If you choose to share it, the iOS share sheet sends it to the destination you select. Shared materials can include travel dates, places, notes, and your character; check the content before sending. The recipient's service then handles it under its own terms.

Support messages

If you email support@logihope.com, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use that information to answer your request and keep support records only as long as reasonably needed for that purpose.

Website access data

These policy and support pages are static pages intended for GitHub Pages hosting. The site itself does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical request information as the hosting provider; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Retention and your choices

Your journal remains in app storage until you remove individual entries or remove the app and its data from your device. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings and may include app data. You can contact us for help at support@logihope.com. We cannot delete information held by Apple or another service on your behalf; please use that provider's controls.

Children

WorldPin is a general travel journal and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support email, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The current version and effective date will be posted here. Questions: support@logihope.com.

简要说明：你的旅行记录保存在本机 App 内。WorldPin 目前没有接收旅行日记的账户服务或自有服务器。使用地图和购买功能时，由 Apple 提供相应服务。

你填写的信息

你可以在 WorldPin 中记录去过的国家和地区、行程地点、日期、笔记、出发地和心愿地点。App 也会保存你选择的旅行分身、语言和外观等设置。这些记录保存在你设备上的 App 本地数据中。

位置与地图

当前版本不会请求读取设备的精确位置。目的地由你自行填写。地图页面使用 App 预设的地理点，并通过 Apple MapKit 显示地图内容。Apple 可能按照Apple 隐私政策处理提供地图服务所需的技术信息。

购买

一次性 Passport 购买和恢复购买由 Apple App Store 与 StoreKit 处理。WorldPin 会读取购买权益状态以启用对应功能。我们不会收到完整的支付卡号。购买信息的处理方式请参见Apple 隐私政策。

分享与导出

旅行卡片、海报、GIF 和 HTML 旅行册由 App 在设备本地生成。你选择分享时，iOS 分享面板会将文件交给你选定的目标。分享内容可能包含旅行日期、地点、笔记和旅行分身；发送前请检查内容。接收方服务会依据其自身条款处理这些内容。

支持邮件

如果你向 support@logihope.com 发送邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和你主动提供的内容，并仅为回复和处理支持请求而使用；相关记录只在合理必要的期限内保留。

网站访问信息

本隐私政策与支持页面是为 GitHub Pages 托管准备的静态页面。网站本身不使用广告、分析工具或追踪 Cookie。GitHub 作为托管服务商可能处理技术访问信息，详情请见 GitHub 隐私声明。

保存期限与管理方式

旅行记录会留在 App 本地，直到你删除单条记录，或从设备移除 App 及其数据。设备备份由你的 Apple 设置控制，备份可能包含 App 数据。需要帮助时，请联系 support@logihope.com。Apple 或其他服务持有的信息需通过对应服务提供的管理方式处理。

儿童

WorldPin 是面向一般用户的旅行记录工具，并非为收集儿童个人信息而设计。如果你认为儿童向我们的支持邮箱发送了个人信息，请联系我们核查。

政策更新与联系

当 App 功能或信息处理方式变化时，我们可能更新本政策。最新版本和生效日期会发布在此页面。联系邮箱：support@logihope.com。
