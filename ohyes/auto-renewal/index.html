自动续费协议 / Auto-Renewal Agreement

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日 · Effective October 21, 2026

订阅内容与价格

年度会员在订阅有效期间解锁完整学习音频。订阅周期为一年。具体价格、当地货币和当期权益会在你确认购买前显示，并以 Apple 付款确认页面为准。

自动续费与扣款

购买确认后，费用将从你的 Apple 账户扣除。除非你在当前订阅周期结束前取消，订阅会自动续订，并在续订时按当时适用价格扣款。Apple 会按其规则处理价格变更通知及需要的确认。

取消与管理

你可以在 iPhone“设置”中的 Apple 账户“订阅”页面管理或取消订阅。取消后，已付费权益通常可使用至当前订阅周期结束；取消不会自动退还已支付的周期费用。退款申请由 Apple 处理。

恢复购买

若更换设备或权益未显示，请在猫头鹰英语“会员与课程”页面点击“恢复购买”，并使用购买时的 Apple 账户。

Subscription and Price

Annual membership unlocks the complete learning audio while the subscription is active. The subscription period is one year. The price, local currency, and current benefits are shown before you confirm and are governed by Apple’s purchase confirmation sheet.

Automatic Renewal and Billing

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Unless you cancel before the current subscription period ends, the subscription automatically renews and your account is charged at the then-current price. Apple handles price-change notices and any required consent under its policies.

Manage or Cancel

Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings on your iPhone: Apple Account > Subscriptions. After cancellation, paid benefits generally remain available through the end of the current period; cancellation does not automatically refund a period already paid for. Apple handles refund requests.

Restore Purchases

If you change devices or your benefits do not appear, tap “Restore Purchases” on the Membership and Courses screen in Owl English and use the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
