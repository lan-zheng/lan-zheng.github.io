隐私政策

OhYes 英语希望让你安心练习。这份说明会告诉你，学习记录、录音和购买信息分别如何处理。

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日

无需注册账号应用不要求创建 OhYes 账号。
学习内容保存在设备进度、收藏、词本和录音由你在本机管理。
不做广告追踪OhYes 不使用广告跟踪或第三方分析 SDK。

我们会处理哪些信息

OhYes 不要求注册或登录，也不运营用于收集学习记录的账号服务器。应用在设备上保存你使用功能所需的内容，例如学习进度、收藏的表达、单词本、偏好设置、你主动录制并保存的练习音频，以及你导入的练习视频。

这些内容用于提供学习、复习、播放、录音和备份功能。OhYes 团队不会通过应用自动接收这些本机内容。

麦克风与录音

只有当你主动开始跟读录音时，应用才会请求麦克风权限。录音在设备上完成；你可以回放、保存或删除。选择“分享 / 保存到文件”时，系统会把你选中的文件交给你指定的应用或存储服务，该服务的数据处理由其自身政策约束。

拒绝麦克风权限仍可使用听读和其他不需要录音的功能。

本地音频、语音和词典

课程示范音频随应用提供，播放时不需要把课程文本发送到 OhYes 服务器。个别临时文字可调用 Apple 系统语音合成；系统词典由 Apple 提供并按系统设置运行。Apple 系统服务可能按 Apple 自己的政策处理请求。

备份与设备同步

应用没有 OhYes 账号或自建云同步。你可以手动导出学习备份，并自行选择保存位置。iOS 设备备份、iCloud、文件提供商和你主动选择的分享目标由 Apple 或相应服务管理；这些服务可能按照你的系统设置保存数据副本。

购买与订阅

应用内购买由 Apple App Store 处理。Apple 负责付款和账单信息；OhYes 仅通过 StoreKit 获取完成购买、恢复购买和提供相应音频权益所需的交易状态。我们不会获得你的完整支付卡号。有关 Apple 如何处理购买数据，请查看 Apple 隐私政策。

广告、分析与数据出售

OhYes 不展示第三方定向广告，不使用广告追踪标识符或第三方分析 SDK，也不出售个人信息。

保存、删除与儿童

学习记录和录音保留在你的设备上，直到你在应用中删除、重置应用或移除应用。系统备份或你导出的副本可能继续保存在所选位置。OhYes 面向成年学习者，不以儿童为目标用户，也不会有意收集儿童个人信息。

政策更新与联系

功能或适用要求变化时，我们可能更新本政策，并在本页修改生效日期。隐私相关问题或删除请求，请联系 support@logihope.com。

你可以随时在应用的“我的”页面重置本机学习数据。重置不会撤销 Apple App Store 中的购买；订阅可在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中管理。

Privacy Policy

OhYes English is designed to help you practice with peace of mind. This policy explains how learning records, recordings, and purchase information are handled.

Effective date: October 19, 2026

No account requiredOhYes does not ask you to create an account.
Learning data stays on your deviceYou manage progress, saved items, vocabulary, and recordings locally.
No ad trackingOhYes does not use ad tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

Information we handle

OhYes does not require registration or sign-in and does not operate an account server that collects learning records. The app stores information needed for its features on your device, such as learning progress, saved phrases, vocabulary, preferences, practice audio you choose to record and save, and practice videos you import.

This information supports learning, review, playback, recording, and backup features. The OhYes team does not automatically receive this on-device content through the app.

Microphone and recordings

The app requests microphone access only when you choose to start a speaking recording. Recording takes place on your device. You can play, save, or delete recordings. If you choose to share or save a file, iOS passes the selected file to the app or storage service you choose; that service handles it under its own policy.

You can deny microphone access and continue to use listening and other features that do not need recording.

Offline audio, speech, and dictionary

Course example audio is included with the app, so playing it does not send course text to an OhYes server. Apple system speech synthesis may be used for some temporary text. The system dictionary is provided by Apple and operates under your device settings. Apple system services may handle requests under Apple’s own policies.

Backups and device sync

The app has no OhYes account or custom cloud sync. You can manually export a learning backup and choose where to save it. iOS device backups, iCloud, file providers, and sharing destinations you select are operated by Apple or the relevant service; they may retain copies according to your system settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

In-app purchases are processed by Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment and billing information. OhYes receives only the StoreKit transaction state needed to complete or restore a purchase and provide the related audio access. We do not receive your full payment card number. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for details about Apple’s data handling.

Advertising, analytics, and sale of data

OhYes does not show third-party targeted ads, use advertising identifiers or third-party analytics SDKs, or sell personal information.

Retention, deletion, and children

Learning records and recordings remain on your device until you delete them, reset the app, or remove the app. System backups or copies you exported may remain in the location you selected. OhYes is designed for adult learners, is not directed to children, and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact support@logihope.com.

You can reset on-device learning data at any time from the Profile tab. Resetting does not cancel App Store purchases. Manage subscriptions in your Apple account settings.
