常见问题

音频可以离线播放吗？

课程示范音频随 OhYes 一起安装。下载完成后，可在没有网络时播放固定课程音频。

如何恢复购买或管理订阅？

打开应用“我的”页面中的“会员与课程方案”，选择“恢复购买”。管理或取消自动续订，请前往 iPhone“设置”中的 Apple 账户订阅页面。Apple 负责处理 App Store 付款和退款申请。

录音和学习进度会上传吗？

OhYes 不要求账号，也不提供自建云同步。练习录音、学习进度和收藏保存在设备上。只有你主动分享或导出时，文件才会交给你选择的目标应用或服务。更多信息见隐私政策。

如何删除学习数据和录音？

可在应用内删除单条内容，或在“我的”页面选择“重置应用”清除本机学习数据。手动导出的文件、分享副本和系统备份需要在对应位置另行删除。

购买后仍显示未解锁怎么办？

先确认 iPhone 使用的是购买时的 Apple 账户，再回到“会员与课程方案”选择“恢复购买”。如果问题仍在，请邮件联系我们；请不要发送密码、完整支付卡号或不必要的私人录音。

联系邮箱：support@logihope.com · 请勿在邮件中发送 Apple 账户密码或完整付款信息。

Common questions

Can I play audio offline?

Course example audio is included with OhYes. Once the app is installed, fixed course audio can play without a network connection.

How do I restore a purchase or manage a subscription?

Open “Membership and plans” from the Profile tab and choose “Restore purchases.” To manage or cancel auto-renewal, open your Apple Account subscriptions in iPhone Settings. Apple handles App Store payments and refund requests.

Are recordings and learning progress uploaded?

OhYes does not require an account or provide custom cloud sync. Practice recordings, progress, and saved items stay on your device. Files are shared with another app or service only when you choose to export or share them. See our Privacy Policy.

How can I delete learning data and recordings?

Delete individual items in the app, or choose “Reset app” in the Profile tab to clear on-device learning data. Manually exported files, shared copies, and system backups must be deleted from their respective locations.

My purchase is not showing as unlocked. What should I do?

Make sure your iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase, then choose “Restore purchases” in “Membership and plans.” If the issue continues, email us. Do not send passwords, full payment card numbers, or private recordings unless they are necessary.

Email: support@logihope.com · Never send your Apple Account password or full payment details by email.
